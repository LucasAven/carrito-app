# The daily ledger loop is free forever; reflection and automation are a one-time Pro unlock

The app must keep paying for itself without nagging the people it was built for, so we split features along a single line: **the daily loop a vendor needs to keep books at all is free forever, and everything that is reflection, automation, or portability is "Pro", unlocked once.** Free is creating, editing, and soft-deleting a [Sale](../CONTEXT.md) or [Expense](../CONTEXT.md), seeing the [Balance](../../app/balance/page.tsx) across its day/week/month/year scopes, the jump-to-date picker, the payment-type filter, and undo — the whole capture-and-check habit that hooks a new Operator and that mom and aunt live in. Pro is the [Resumen](../v2-stats-plan.md) stats page, the [Export](./0008-export-via-print-and-client-csv.md) report (`/exportar` CSV/PDF), Siri voice entry via the token API (see [ADR-0004](./0004-siri-entry-via-shortcut-api.md)), and multi-business if it ever lands (see [v2-wishlist](../v2-wishlist.md)). The principle, not the list, is load-bearing: when a new feature arrives, ask "does a vendor need this to keep their books, or does it help them reflect on / automate / move those books once they're already committed?" — the former is free, the latter is Pro. The one-time [legacy import](../legacy-import-plan.md) is neither; it is a hand-run admin backfill, never a user-facing surface, so it sits outside this boundary entirely.

Gating is one server-checked flag on the Operator, `operators.is_pro` (lifetime, not a recurring entitlement), flipped to `true` by a Mercado Pago payment webhook — chosen because Argentines are fleeing recurring charges toward one-time / QR payments and because a lifetime unlock has no churn, no dunning, and no monthly settlement to reconcile. Pro features are **shown but locked** (an "Activar Pro" prompt in place of the gated action) rather than hidden, so the upgrade is discoverable from inside the free habit instead of buried. Existing Operators — mom and aunt — are grandfathered to `is_pro = true`, so monetization never degrades the people the app was built for. A Cafecito tip jar is orthogonal goodwill, gates nothing, and is not part of this boundary.

We rejected a subscription: Mercado Pago Suscripciones is ~10–11% all-in on instant payouts, carries churn and AR consumer distrust of recurring debits, and asks a price-sensitive informal vendor for an ongoing commitment they won't make. We rejected ads outright (they would wreck the [fluffless](../../readme.md) utility and were ruled out from the start). We rejected gating any part of the daily loop: taxing the capture habit kills adoption and would charge mom and aunt for the thing the app exists to do. We rejected à-la-carte per-feature unlocks as too fiddly for coffee-money scale. This boundary is deliberately tuned for goodwill and low friction over revenue extraction, because the bounding economics make that the only honest target: charging at all moves the app off Vercel Hobby's non-commercial terms (migrate hosting to a commercial-OK host first), and monotributo's fixed monthly cuota means realistic income here is "pays for the domain and buys some coffees", not a salary. Revisit the free/Pro line — or the one-time-vs-recurring choice — only if the Operator base grows well past the family-and-friends scale this assumes.
