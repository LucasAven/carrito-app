# Extraordinary expenses are a flag on the Entry and sit outside every total

A food truck buys a gas bottle or fills the car with fuel every few weeks, and those spends are large next to a day of coffee sales, so counting them in Egresos made an ordinary day read as a loss and pulled the Resumen averages, the record day and the best-day/best-month panels down with it. The Operator still wants to see them, so an Expense now carries an `extraordinary` boolean ([migration](../../supabase/migrations/20261026120000_entries_extraordinary.sql), with a check constraint so only an Expense can set it) and that money is reported as its own figure instead of being mixed in: [`summarizeEntries`](../../lib/db/entries.ts) splits it out of Egresos, the Balance net is Sales minus ordinary expenses only, and [`computeStats`](../../lib/stats/aggregate.ts) keeps it out of `totalExpenses`, `net`, the per-worked-day average, the record and both bar panels, under "Bruto" and "Neto" alike. The [Balance card](../../components/Balance/index.tsx) gives it one line below the Ingresos/Egresos row, always rendered even at `$ 0` (a zero reads as "nothing extraordinary this month", a missing line reads as a bug), covering the whole calendar month in day and month view, the year in year view, and the exact span in week and custom-range view. That line **ignores the payment-type filter** while Ingresos and Egresos keep respecting it: the filter exists to reconcile cash-on-hand against the Mercado Pago balance, and a sporadic spend is something the Operator wants to remember happened rather than an amount to reconcile, so filtering it would only ever show half of it. We rejected a third `entry_kind`, which would have forced every existing read, tab and filter to learn a new case, where a flag leaves all of them working and changes only the totals. We rejected leaving these spends unlogged, which is what the Operator did before and is why the books never matched the bank. Revisit if the sporadic spends grow numerous enough to want categories of their own (fuel, gas, repairs), which would be a column beside the flag rather than a change to this decision.
